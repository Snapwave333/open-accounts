# Creator Earnings & Payouts

THIS POLICY SETS OUT THE TERMS UNDER WHICH CREATORS ARE PAID FOR PAID-FOR SERVICES, INCLUDING PLATFORM COMMISSIONS, WITHDRAWALS, AND ASSOCIATED FEES.

This Creator Earnings & Payout (the "Policy") forms part of your agreement with Shift Holdings Ltd (“Fanvue”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) and applies to your use of the Platform as a Creator.

It supplements the [Creator Terms & Conditions](/creator-terms) and the [General Terms & Conditions](/terms-conditions).

All defined terms used herein shall have the meaning assigned in the [**General Terms & Conditions**](/terms-conditions), unless otherwise stated.

Please read this Policy carefully. By using the Platform as a Creator, you agree to the terms of this Policy. If you do not agree, you must not provide Paid-for Services on the Platform.

---

## **DEFINITIONS**

"**Creator**" means a User who has completed Creator onboarding and is authorised to submit Paid-for Services or earn revenue through the Platform.

"**Creator Earnings**" means the portion of revenue from Paid-for Services that a Creator is entitled to receive after deduction of Platform Fees and applicable adjustments.

"**Platform Fees**" means the portion of revenue retained by Fanvue for provision of the Platform, services, and operational overheads.

"**Payout**" means the disbursement of Creator Earnings from your Fanvue account to your chosen withdrawal method.

"**Pending Period**" means the minimum time that must pass after a User transaction before those funds become eligible for withdrawal.

## 1. **CREATOR EARNINGS STRUCTURE**

1. Creators are entitled to receive Creator Earnings on all qualifying Paid-for Services transacted via the Platform, including subscriptions, tips, pay-per-view messages, bundles, and custom content.
2. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the standard **Creator Earning Rate** is **80%** of gross revenue from Paid-for Services, with the remaining **20%** retained by Fanvue as Platform Fees.
3. From time to time, Fanvue may offer a **Promotional Earning Rate**: a temporary, fixed-period uplift to the standard Creator Earning Rate (for example, an increased creator share for newly-onboarded creators during an introductory window). The applicable rate, eligibility criteria, and duration of any Promotional Earning Rate are published on the Platform and may be introduced, updated, extended, reduced, or withdrawn at Fanvue's discretion without amendment to this Policy. Any Promotional Earning Rate in force at the time of a qualifying transaction will apply automatically.
4. We may also adjust the Creator Earning Rate for partnership agreements on a case-by-case basis. Any such changes will be communicated in writing.
5. Creator Earnings are calculated exclusive of taxes, fees, or third-party processing charges and may be subject to adjustment for refunds, chargebacks, or fraud-related deductions.

## 2. **WITHDRAWALS & PAYOUT PROCESS**

1. You may request a Payout of your available Creator Balance at any time, provided that:
   1. The funds have cleared the applicable **Pending Period** following each User transaction; and
   2. The withdrawal meets the minimum threshold applicable to your chosen withdrawal method.
2. The standard Pending Period is **seven (7) days**, but this may be extended up to **twenty-eight (28) days** depending on factors such as account verification and user activity. The applicable Pending Period is determined by our internal fraud and risk systems, and is subject to change without notice.
3. Payouts are processed via the withdrawal method registered in your Creator Account. Supported methods may include:
   1. Direct bank transfer;
   2. Crypto wallets (where supported);
   3. Third-party wallets/services.
4. All Payouts are subject to standard anti-fraud, compliance, and payment provider checks.
5. We aim to initiate all valid Payout requests within **ten (10) Business Days** of receipt, though this may be delayed by:
   1. Pending fraud or content investigations;
   2. Banking or third-party delays;
   3. Issues with your payment details.
6. You are solely responsible for ensuring the accuracy and validity of your payment information. We are not liable for failed Payouts due to incorrect or outdated details.
7. If you do not receive a Payout or believe an amount is incorrect, you must notify us **within 30 days** of the associated transaction. Beyond this period, no adjustments will be made.
8. **Dormant Balances & Unclaimed Earnings**
   1. **Scope.** This section applies to your Creator Balance (net of fees/adjustments) that is eligible for payout and reflects amounts contractually owed by Fanvue for your Services.
   2. **UK/EEA creators.**
      1. If you do not (i) request a Payout or (ii) submit a valid invoice (where required) for any part of your Creator Balance within twelve (12) months of the date the relevant Services were provided, that portion of your balance will be classified as Dormant.
      2. We will send at least two reminders (for example, at \~9 months and \~11 months) to your registered email and in-product before Dormant classification.
      3. After classification, you are deemed to have irrevocably waived your right to payment of the Dormant amount and we may close the balance and retain it.
      4. However, we may, at our sole discretion, reinstate and pay a late claim made within six (6) years of accrual, subject to compliance checks.
   3. **US creators (or balances subject to US unclaimed property law).**
      1. If your last known address or applicable law is in the United States, we will classify the balance as Dormant after twelve (12) months of no payout or invoice activity and perform all required due diligence outreach.
      2. If you do not respond before the applicable state dormancy period expires (typically 3-5 years, state-dependent), we will report and remit the balance to the appropriate state unclaimed property authority.
      3. You may later reclaim it from that authority. Contract terms cannot waive these statutory obligations.
   4. **Rest of World creators (non-UK/EEA/US).**
      1. If you are not located in the UK, EEA, or United States, the same terms as UK/EEA creators apply: twelve (12) month deadline with reminders, after which you waive payment rights.
      2. We may, at our sole discretion, reinstate and pay late claims within six (6) years, subject to compliance checks.
      3. If your local law mandates specific unclaimed property procedures (similar to US escheat laws), we will comply with those requirements instead of this provision.
   5. **Self-billing.** Where permitted, we may issue self-billed invoices on your behalf for VAT/tax compliance. Self-billing does not alter Dormant classification or the timelines above.
   6. **No trust or client money relationship.** Creator Balances are contractual receivables owed by Fanvue; they are not held on trust, not client money, and not electronic money or deposits. This does not limit any mandatory safeguarding or escheat obligations that apply in your jurisdiction.
   7. **How to reactivate.** You can reactivate a Dormant balance at any time before the applicable deadline (six years in the UK/EEA and Rest of World; the state's statutory period in the US) by updating required tax/KYC and submitting a payout request; we may require additional verification.

## 3. **TAX RESPONSIBILITIES**

1. Creator Earnings are paid gross of tax. You are responsible for determining, reporting, and paying all taxes due in your jurisdiction in respect of your earnings from the Platform.
2. We may collect or withhold taxes where required by law and will provide relevant documentation (e.g. self-billing invoices, VAT invoices, 1099's etc.) where applicable.
3. You agree to provide accurate tax documentation when requested and understand that failure to do so may result in delayed or withheld Payouts.

## 4. **REFUNDS, CHARGEBACKS & ADJUSTMENTS**

1. If a User successfully receives a refund or initiates a chargeback for Paid-for Services related to your account, we may:
   1. Deduct the disputed amount from your Creator Balance;
   2. Withhold it from future Payouts if already paid out;
   3. Recover it from your payment provider in the event of a platform breach.
2. If your account exceeds a **1.5% chargeback or dispute rate** in any rolling 30-day period, we may impose a **\$50 USD** (FIFTY US DOLLARS) administrative fee for each additional dispute. This fee is discretionary and may be waived in genuine cases of error or attempted resolution.
